Research method

Evidence before interpretation

The Gallia project is built around contemporary documents rather than later retellings alone.

Catalogues document what a company offered and how it presented its products. Advertisements can establish that an event was announced, but not necessarily that it occurred. Later bibliographies and histories are useful guides to original sources, but their claims are checked wherever possible.

Archive identifiers beginning with GAL are internal research references. They are not the holding institution’s shelfmarks.

Dating and attribution

The year embedded in an internal file identifier does not, by itself, establish a publication date. Undated items are described as undated until verified against the document or institutional catalogue.

Names such as Société Gallia and Félix Boudou & Cie are transcribed as they appear in the cited records. Corporate continuity and ownership are separate research questions.

Primary sources

The following records are already documented in the project archive.

GAL-002

Gallia. Catalogue général et liste des prix n° 22

Date: Undated; publication year not independently verified. Language: French. Holding institution: Bibliothèques spécialisées et patrimoniales de la Ville de Paris, ARK pf0002154401.

Digitised file of 24 pages, including catalogue spreads and supplementary material. The archival description identifies Gallia-Boncelle and Gallia Métro permanent-wave apparatus, drying and washing equipment, salon chairs and furnishings, preparations, accessories, chair price lists, a promotional letter and references to the Gallia-Journal. The cover states that this edition replaces earlier catalogues.

GAL-003

Gallia advertisement in Fodrász Újság

Date: April 1929. Language: Hungarian. Source: Fodrász Újság, digitised by the Hungarian Electronic Periodicals Archive.

Announcement of Gallia lectures and permanent-wave-machine demonstrations in Budapest during the first half of April 1929. The notice refers to a Gallia-school instructor and the director of the export department. It is evidence of a planned promotional and training visit; completion of the events has not been independently verified.

Related material

Further documentary leads

The project also holds a consolidated research register based on French hairdressing-periodical material from 1920. It identifies G. and F. Boudou in connection with a French professional hairdressing enterprise. The relevant original pages should be checked before precise claims about company structure or individual roles are published.

Trade-journal references to Gallia-Journal and other contemporary professional publications offer promising leads, but they are not presented here as fully examined sources.

Images and reproduction

Links lead to the holding institutions’ records. No claim is made that digitised images are free to reproduce. Illustrations will be added only after their provenance and applicable reproduction conditions have been checked.
