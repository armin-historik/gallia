Catalogues & Advertising

What Gallia offered, how it presented its products, and where its publications circulated.

Historical catalogues and trade advertisements provide direct evidence of Gallia's professional equipment, commercial language and international promotion. This page identifies the sources and makes clear what remains uncertain.

Catalogue No. 22Browse records
Reading the evidence

A business beyond permanent waving

Gallia's surviving commercial material documents a broad range of equipment for the professional hairdressing salon.

Catalogue No. 22 records not only permanent-wave apparatus but also drying equipment, washing and antiseptic installations, furniture, chairs, preparations and accessories. These are important clues to the company's wider commercial activities.

The entries below distinguish catalogue content described in our archival records from information that still needs checking in the original PDFs.

Dating caution

The number of a catalogue is not a publication year. Neither Catalogue No. 22 nor the catalogue bearing the slogan La Marque de la Qualité is assigned a verified year here.

Our internal identifier GAL-1936-001 is not, by itself, proof that the second catalogue appeared in 1936.

Primary source records

The documented Gallia publications

GAL-002 · Date unverified · French

Catalogue général et liste des prix n° 22

Gallia · General catalogue and price list No. 22 · Paris

The digitised 24-page file contains illustrated catalogue material together with supplementary price lists and a promotional letter. According to the project's source record, the cover states that this edition supersedes previous catalogues.

Documented product groups

  • Permanent-wave apparatus, including the names Gallia-Boncelle and Gallia Métro
  • Hair dryers and central drying installations
  • Washing and antiseptic equipment
  • Salon furniture, barber and ladies' chairs, and children's seats
  • Waving preparations, tools and accessories
  • Promotional material and references to the Gallia-Journal

What this establishes: Gallia marketed an integrated range of professional salon equipment. What it does not yet establish: the introduction dates or detailed operating principles of the two named permanent-wave systems.

GAL-1936-001 · Date unverified · French

Gallia — La Marque de la Qualité

Société Gallia / Félix Boudou & Cie · Paris

The archival record identifies a historical Gallia catalogue cover with a stylised hairstyle, the Gallia name and the slogan La Marque de la Qualité (“The Mark of Quality”). The cover gives the company address as 8, rue Notre-Dame-de-Lorette, Paris.

This source helps document Gallia's visual identity and commercial presentation. A detailed product inventory and publication date must be checked against the complete digitised catalogue and its bibliographic record.

GAL-003 · April 1929 · Hungarian

Gallia demonstrations advertised in Budapest

Fodrász Újság · April 1929 · Trade advertisement

A Hungarian-language advertisement announces lectures and demonstrations of Gallia permanent-wave machines in Budapest during the first half of April 1929. It mentions an instructor associated with the Gallia school, an export department director and a local contact at Rákóczi út 14.

Evidence boundary: the notice establishes that the events were advertised. It does not independently prove that the demonstrations took place.

Source interpretation

What these documents tell us

Equipment and services

Catalogue No. 22 provides evidence for a varied professional range. It allows Gallia to be studied as a salon-equipment supplier rather than solely as a permanent-wave brand.

Brand identity

The slogan La Marque de la Qualité and the Paris address provide contemporary clues to the company's self-presentation.

Professional communication

The catalogue's reference to the Gallia-Journal points toward a company-associated publication that deserves separate examination.

International promotion

The Budapest advertisement provides a dated example of planned demonstrations outside France, with training and export roles mentioned in the notice.

Research in progress

Questions for the next catalogue review

When the complete PDF files are examined page by page, the next priorities are to transcribe model names and specifications, record price-list dates and currencies, identify captions and illustrations, and compare the company addresses and names across editions.
